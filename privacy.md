# StackWorks privacy policy

_Effective 8 October 2026_

StackWorks is a stacking game for Android published by Digimoxie. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. In short: nothing leaves your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. StackWorks does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't use the network.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your game progress (unlocked levels, best heights, stars, achievements) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion) on your phone so they're there next time. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

## Permissions

StackWorks uses vibration for haptic feedback. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other personal information.

## Children

StackWorks collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Google Play

You download StackWorks through Google Play, which handles installs, updates and crash reports under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
